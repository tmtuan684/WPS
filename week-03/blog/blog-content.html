A Taste of Tuscany: Homemade Pasta Secrets

By Maria Rossi • March 5, 2024 • Tags: Italian Pasta Basics

Making fresh pasta at home is simpler than it seems. With just flour, eggs, and a little patience, you’ll craft silky strands that outshine anything from a box.

Ingredients

  • 200g “00” flour (or all-purpose)
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Semolina for dusting (optional)

Method

  1. Make a well: On a clean surface, mound flour and make a well. Crack eggs into the center, add salt.
  2. Combine: Beat eggs with a fork, gradually pulling flour in until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Knead: Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. If sticky, add a dusting of flour.
  4. Rest: Wrap and rest 30 minutes at room temperature.
  5. Roll: Use a pin or machine; start thick, finish thin (about 1–1.5mm).
  6. Shape: Cut into tagliatelle, pappardelle, or make ravioli (see below).
  7. Cook: Boil in salted water 2–3 minutes, until just tender.

Note: Resting lets gluten relax so the dough rolls thinner and cooks evenly.
